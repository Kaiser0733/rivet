## Honest gaps

- **§18 remains open.** The observed build, WebView version, and HTML that had the visible heading would be needed to distinguish an app compositor issue from the served page or an earlier viewport state. Live Android runs are also needed for the browser-card pixels and close gesture, camera/photo/document pickers and SAF grants, long-press toolbar and outside-tap behavior, wide folds and landscape Recents, and inline-image placement. A host compile can't verify any of these interactions.
- The archive trigger handles recognizable recall phrasings. It cannot detect every implicit reference in free text. The automatic response quotes evidence instead of asking a model to fill gaps. Each search and wire tail is bounded. All saved quotes remain durable, and the latest eight enter the request so repeated retrieval cannot fill a small model's context. Legacy event rows have their original order and turn labels, but no invented timestamps. Provider-side cache hits and the 14-turn phone repro are pending.
- The meter shares Rivet's request estimator. Provider-specific tokenization, vision charge and invisible provider framing can differ from the estimate.
- The Termux envelope fits the replacement receiver. A real phone run is needed to verify delivery from Termux through Android and to identify the precise point where the old run dropped the broadcast.
