# Rivet on a phone · Soul batch implementation plan

Planning base `7d28b6c8` · audit `origin/soul-audit:report.md` at `626f7973`. Evidence is the audit, the founder’s verified composer finding, and the fourteen supplied pictures. Kotlin paths below are relative to `app/src/main/java/com/kaiser/rivet/`. “Gate” below means evidence to collect during implementation. No test suites or builds were run for this plan.

## 1. Direction

**Workbench companion.** Adopt the audit’s recommendation. The person using Rivet needs to distinguish what they asked for, what Rivet is actually doing and when the work comes back to them. Give that last moment one recognizable gesture inside the existing turn fold. Let ordinary Android controls, readable names and the project’s own words carry the rest.

Chat stays in its current slot with its existing header, empty-state block, folds, composer and footer.

Use FastOutSlowIn for the 180 ms fold open, 150 ms fold close and the one 180 ms outcome reveal. Retain the end panel’s existing 220 ms open / 160 ms close timing and easing for gestures. No pulse during waits, arrival animation on historical messages, haptic, or sound. On review, the photos favor preserving the folder doodle and tool labels over adding a branded welcome card. The only authored flourish is the transition back from a live turn.

## 5. Device acceptance round

- **First minute.** Exercise no provider, no project, ready and revoked folder as separate states. Check the offline and starter routes as well as reconnecting the same folder. With full YOLO selected, read the ready text aloud and check that it makes no unconditional approval promise.
- **Same chat.** Compare before/after at the same state, density, orientation, font and keyboard setting. The header, fold, scroll anchor, composer, footer and empty-state block keep their positions. Check both themes, System mode and large text. Read metadata outdoors or at low brightness. Check focus rings, selected state and switch contrast.
- **Two plus buttons.** Start with a sentence in an active draft. Attach a photo and a document through their respective system pickers in separate trials. Check the intended session, original sentence, filename/image and exactly one staged result. Cancel both pickers, rotate during return, try an unreadable or rejected document, and verify explicit send. Compare to sharing the same items from another app, including a text-only model’s truthful image note. Try empty and populated clipboard. Confirm only its tap reads text and the header + retains its original purpose.
- **Panel and recovery.** Open Tools via its announced name. Close with its X, Back and scrim. Open the terminal via its existing card. Compare the header while a real command runs and after it exits. Inspect a long actual checkpoint, timestamp, Restore target and Undo confirmation. Use a disposable project for recovery checks.
- **Hands and speech.** With TalkBack and touch exploration, reach Tools close, fold chevrons, worker control and history actions. Check effective 48 dp targets where planned, adjacent-target separation and the browser window close. Enter and swipe-type a sentence with the reported IME. Record the IME/device if the gesture fails.
- **During and after work.** Observe genuine waits with detail and without, a worker switch, pause, stop, failure and successful completion. Only the live success gets the one quiet reveal. Reopen history, scroll a row off and on, rotate, and repeat with animations disabled. Read the actual outcome each time. Check for jumps, repeated announcements and exposed hidden reasoning. Record any mid-reply language drift as transcript evidence.
- **Specialist actions.** Use provider setup, local fit, browser empty/loaded, phone capture consent and notification, starter selection and replay export/error on a phone. Compare every next-action label to the actual destination. Keep actual settings, notices, watermark and safety boundaries visible. Capture paired before/after frames for Hermes, including a short recording of the completion transition at normal and zero motion.
